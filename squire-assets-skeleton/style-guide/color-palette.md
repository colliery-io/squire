# Squire Color Palette

Version: 1.1

Purpose:

This document defines the official Squire color system and establishes how color communicates hierarchy, rewards, rarity, progression, and emotional state throughout the application.

Goals:

* Child-friendly
* Fantasy themed
* Highly readable
* Warm and welcoming
* Accessible for parents
* Consistent with familiar RPG progression systems

---

# Design Philosophy

Color should communicate meaning before the user reads text.

Players should immediately understand:

* What is valuable
* What is rare
* What is a reward
* What is positive
* What requires attention

The palette should feel magical and adventurous without becoming loud, saturated, or overwhelming.

# Reward & Rarity System

This system is intentionally modeled after familiar RPG/MMORPG progression systems.

Color alone should help users understand relative value.

---

## Common

Role:

Everyday quests and rewards.

Color:
Forest Green

#5D8A42

Examples:

* Basic chores
* Daily tasks
* Starter badges

---

## Uncommon

Role:

Above-average rewards.

Color:
Castle Blue

#4E79A7

Examples:

* Weekly rewards
* Bonus quests
* Milestone tasks

---

## Rare

Role:

Special rewards.

Color:
Royal Purple

#5A3D8E

Examples:

* Special badges
* Rare rewards
* Achievement unlocks

---

## Epic

Role:

High-value rewards.

Color:
Orange Gold

Base:
#E08B2D

Light:
#F2A74B

Dark:
#B86816

Examples:

* Major rewards
* Long streak milestones
* Exceptional achievements
* Premium quest chains

Important:

Epic uses Orange Gold, not Adventure Gold.

Adventure Gold is reserved for currency and progression.

---

## Legendary

Role:

Highest achievement tier.

Primary:
Royal Purple

Secondary:
Orange Gold

Accent:
Adventure Gold

Examples:

* Legendary badges
* Major account milestones
* Long-term accomplishments
* Seasonal achievements

Legendary assets should combine:

* Purple
* Orange
* Gold

in a balanced composition.

No other rarity tier should use all three simultaneously.

# Accessibility Rules

Minimum contrast target:

WCAG AA

Required:

Text should never appear directly on:

* Gold
* Purple
* Orange
* Detailed textures

without a backing surface.

Preferred text colors:

Primary:
Ink (#3C2D21)

Secondary:
Stone (#8C857B)

# Future Expansion

Additional rarity tiers should be introduced only if required.

Recommended order:

Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary

Do not introduce Mythic, Artifact, Ascended, Ancient, or other tiers unless progression systems require them.

Maintaining rarity clarity is more important than increasing rarity count.
